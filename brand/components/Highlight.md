# Highlight

The highlighter: a `highlight` background behind `ink` text that marks the one thing a reader must not miss on the screen.

- One per view. If two things compete, the second one isn't a highlight; rewrite it or move it up.
- Good uses: the decision a checkpoint is asking for, the riskiest assumption in a critique, the line that changed in a revision.
- A phrase or a sentence, never a paragraph, a heading, a button, or a banner.
- Never put `action` links inside a highlight, and never use it to mean "warning". Draftt has no warning yellow.
